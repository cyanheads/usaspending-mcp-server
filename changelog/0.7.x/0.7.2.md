# 0.7.2 — 2026-11-12

## Added

- **`usaspending_autocomplete_filters` `type: "location"`** resolves US state, county, and congressional district names to the codes the location filters and `shape_codes` take. `FM`, `MH`, `PW`, and `UM` have no upstream state row and never get a guessed code. ([#108](https://github.com/cyanheads/usaspending-mcp-server/issues/108))
- **`filters.place_of_performance_location` and `filters.recipient_location`** on `usaspending_spending_by_category`, `usaspending_spending_by_geography`, `usaspending_spending_over_time`, and the nested `filters` of `usaspending_search_awards`, echoed as `applied_location` and `applied_recipient_location`; a bad location fails as `invalid_location`. ([#109](https://github.com/cyanheads/usaspending-mcp-server/issues/109))
- **`USASPENDING_CLAMP_LIMITS`** (default `true`) reduces a result-count `limit` above a tool's cap to the cap, disclosed as `limitClamp` plus a notice. `false` advertises each cap as the schema `maximum` and rejects above it; under it `usaspending_get_award_transactions` accepts up to 2,000 and `usaspending_search_recipients` up to 1,000 (both were 100). ([#99](https://github.com/cyanheads/usaspending-mcp-server/issues/99))
- **`usaspending_get_recipient`** returns `parent_id` and `parents[]`; a `-P` parent also returns `children` and `children_total`, sized by the new `children_limit` (default 50, at most 500 under the same switch). A failed children request leaves the profile intact and is named in `unavailable_sections`, as is a `-P` profile with neither UEI nor DUNS (`upstream_error`, not retryable). ([#110](https://github.com/cyanheads/usaspending-mcp-server/issues/110))
- **`usaspending_get_agency` `budget_by_fiscal_year`** lists budgetary resources, obligations, outlays, and `reported_through_period` for every fiscal year with data since FY2017, newest first. ([#111](https://github.com/cyanheads/usaspending-mcp-server/issues/111))
- **`usaspending_get_award`** returns `competition` and `contract_pricing` on contracts and IDVs, `county_code` on both locations, and `total_subaward_amount`. ([#112](https://github.com/cyanheads/usaspending-mcp-server/issues/112))
- **`usaspending_spending_by_category`** adds `federal_account` and `defc` categories (rows overlap and never sum to a total) and `agency_slug` on agency rows. ([#113](https://github.com/cyanheads/usaspending-mcp-server/issues/113))
- **`usaspending_search_awards`** rows carry `recipient_id` and `uei`, and `page_metadata.total` (also `totalCount`) comes from a best-effort count request; when it fails, `unavailable_sections` names `total`. ([#114](https://github.com/cyanheads/usaspending-mcp-server/issues/114))

## Changed

- **`usaspending_search_awards` `has_next`** is exact on page-number paging once the total is known, and the cursor-pair guidance is dropped whenever page numbers reach every match (a total up to 50,000). The `has_next` output description is corrected to match. ([#114](https://github.com/cyanheads/usaspending-mcp-server/issues/114))
- **`usaspending_spending_by_category`** ranks continue across pages instead of restarting at 1. ([#113](https://github.com/cyanheads/usaspending-mcp-server/issues/113))
- **`usaspending_spending_by_geography` `limit`** has no maximum (was 500).
- **`usaspending_get_idv_awards`** drops its raise-limit hint when `limit` is already at the cap.
- **Single-attempt timeouts** say "within its Nms deadline (one attempt, not retried)" instead of claiming retries.
- **`usaspending-guide` 1.1 → 1.2** covers recipient IDs on search rows, multiple parents, and location lookup.
- **Framework skills, scripts, and agent docs** resynced to `@cyanheads/mcp-ts-core` 0.13.22.
