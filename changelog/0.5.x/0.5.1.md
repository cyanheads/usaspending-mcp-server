# 0.5.1 — 2026-10-24

## Changed

- **Disaster recipient narrowing** accepts trimmed `filters.query`, echoes the applied query, preserves upstream messages, and describes the 10,000 reachable ceiling as a lower bound on possible matches. ([#100](https://github.com/cyanheads/usaspending-mcp-server/issues/100))

## Fixed

- **Disaster overview filters** forward optional DEF codes and echo applied codes; unsupported filters produce notices, and timeout recovery includes filtered requests plus retry and agency-breakdown alternatives. ([#91](https://github.com/cyanheads/usaspending-mcp-server/issues/91))
- **Empty-page recovery** distinguishes zero totals, exhausted pages, empty interior pages, and unknown totals across award/account, recipient, and disaster tools, with notices on both output surfaces. ([#93](https://github.com/cyanheads/usaspending-mcp-server/issues/93))
- **Recipient keywords** are trimmed; blank text omits the keyword filter and discloses the unrestricted keyword search while retaining award-type filters and pagination notices. ([#94](https://github.com/cyanheads/usaspending-mcp-server/issues/94))
- **Validation recovery** declares non-retryable `incomplete_cursor` and `missing_def_codes` reasons with structured recovery hints and matching text. ([#95](https://github.com/cyanheads/usaspending-mcp-server/issues/95))
- **Award performance cities** use `Primary Place of Performance.city_name` instead of a city code, preserving sparse locations. ([#101](https://github.com/cyanheads/usaspending-mcp-server/issues/101))
