# 0.7.1 — 2026-11-03

## Changed

- **`usaspending-guide` 1.0 → 1.1** explains disaster monetary coverage and ranking before pagination.

## Fixed

- **Time-series scope** echoes effective award types and discloses the contract default; breakdown zeros apply only within that selection. ([#105](https://github.com/cyanheads/usaspending-mcp-server/issues/105))
- **Disaster amount coverage** distinguishes complete, partial, unavailable, and unknown monetary scope from matching rows and counts; reference failures retain the primary response. ([#106](https://github.com/cyanheads/usaspending-mcp-server/issues/106))
- **Disaster ranking** orders obligations before pagination, collecting all agency award pages when required under one shared deadline of at most 45 seconds. ([#107](https://github.com/cyanheads/usaspending-mcp-server/issues/107))
- **Analytics recipient filters** reject malformed hashes, normalize hexadecimal case, and echo the applied recipient ID. ([#115](https://github.com/cyanheads/usaspending-mcp-server/issues/115))
- **Effective input validation** checks merged keywords and NAICS codes, normalizes numeric NAICS aliases and disaster DEF codes, advertises supported IDV sorts, and pads short agency codes while preserving four-digit identifiers. ([#116](https://github.com/cyanheads/usaspending-mcp-server/issues/116))
